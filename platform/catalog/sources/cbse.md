# CBSE sources — catalog 2026-27

Every official document used for `platform/catalog/cbse/**` and `platform/catalog/sources/cbse.md` anchors.
All documents were fetched on **2026-10-25** by the curriculum-architect (CBSE). Official PDFs are **not** committed; they were
downloaded to a local scratch folder outside the repo. Each `##` heading's code-formatted id is the anchor that
`subjects.yaml` (`source`) and `syllabus.yaml` (`sources`) refer to.

Originality rule: these documents define **scope and pattern only**. No question, passage, poem or textbook text is copied into the catalog.
