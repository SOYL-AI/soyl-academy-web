# CISCE sources (ICSE / ISC) - academic year 2026-27

Every `source` / `sources` anchor used in `platform/catalog/cisce/**` is defined here.
Publisher of every document: **Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), New Delhi**.
All documents were fetched on **2026-10-25** from cisce.org (direct PDF links; the site's HTML index pages are listed below).
Official PDFs are **not** committed to the repo (copyright CISCE). They were used only to learn scope and pattern (CONTEXT section 8);
no syllabus text, prescribed literature or question is reproduced in the catalog.
