## Scope
- All six syllabus topics (t01–t06) are covered; the chapter has no removed topics in the 2026-27 syllabus.
- Class 12 depth: Mendel's method and seven pea characters; monohybrid, dihybrid and test crosses; laws of dominance, segregation and independent assortment; incomplete dominance (snapdragon), the enzyme view of dominance, co-dominance and multiple alleles (ABO), pleiotropy (phenylketonuria, pea starch gene), polygenic inheritance (skin colour); chromosomal theory (Sutton and Boveri), Morgan's Drosophila, linkage and recombination (1.3 and 37.2 percent), Sturtevant's maps; sex determination (XO, XY, ZW, honey bee haplodiploidy); pedigree symbols; haemophilia and colour blindness; sickle-cell anaemia (Glu → Val, GAG → GUG), phenylketonuria, thalassemia (α on chromosome 16, β on 11); aneuploidy and polyploidy; Down's (trisomy 21), Klinefelter's (47, XXY), Turner's (45, XO).
