## Scope
All four syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml`, with the notes of the ISC 2027 Accountancy syllabus (source `isc-2027-accounts`, read from the CISCE PDF on 2026-10-31) applied throughout:
- **t01** dissolution of partnership vs dissolution of the firm; settlement of accounts under **Section 48** (losses from profits, then capital, then partners in the profit-sharing ratio; assets to third parties, partners' loans, capitals, residue).
- **t02** the Realisation Account: assets at gross value; a **related provision or fund** (doubtful debts, depreciation, investment fluctuation) transferred to Realisation, an unrelated general reserve to capitals; outside liabilities and the **bank loan** through Realisation; sale, takeovers by partners, liabilities assumed by partners, creditors accepting assets (in full or with cash), discounts, unrecorded assets and liabilities; the ISC assumptions for **silent items** (tangible assets and investments at book value; intangibles, accrued income and prepaid expenses nil; liabilities paid in full).
- **t03** realisation expenses in every syllabus case: paid by the firm; paid by a partner; borne (and paid) by a partner, with or without remuneration; to be borne by a partner but paid by the firm; paid by a partner and **reimbursed with a firm's asset**.
- **t04** the **Memorandum Balance Sheet** (incl. a missing balance as the balancing figure), partners' loan accounts, capital accounts and the Bank/Cash Account: **bank overdraft** as the opening credit balance of the Bank Account (never in Realisation), a **partner's loan repaid in cash even when his capital is in debit** (no set-off), a **loan to a partner debited to his capital**, **current accounts transferred to capitals** (fixed capitals), debit balances made good in cash.

Removed for ISC 2027 (admission-cum-retirement, amalgamation, conversion or sale to a company, piecemeal distribution, insolvency of partners) appear only as one "Not in the ISC 2027 syllabus" line in notes section 1. The ways a firm may be dissolved (Sections 40–44) are not listed in the ISC 2027 topic and are not taught, which also keeps insolvency out of the content.

## Blueprint fit (ISC class default 1/2/3/5)
Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. Board dissolution questions are usually one practical (Realisation Account, capital accounts and Bank/Cash Account together, about 8 marks). The default fits by splitting: 2-mark items are two or three entries, a memorandum balance sheet or a distinction; 3-mark items are one account, a Section 48 payout or a set of entries; 5-mark items are set in parts (2 + 3 or 3 + 2). Complete practicals are kept as 8-mark practice items outside the tests. A subject blueprint with an 8-mark practical would fit Accounts better.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Related funds and provisions** (ISC note) go to the Realisation Account: provision for doubtful debts, provision for depreciation and investment fluctuation fund. A workmen compensation reserve is deliberately not used (whether it is "related" without a recorded claim is open to reading).
- **Expenses reimbursed with a firm's asset** are recorded as two entries through the partner's capital (expenses credited, asset debited at the agreed value); the asset is already in the Realisation Account at book value.
- **Bank overdraft** opens the Bank Account on the credit side ("By Balance b/d (bank overdraft)"); no journal entry is shown for it.
- **Creditors partly settled with an asset** (Sualkuchi): the Realisation debit "Bank A/c (Creditors paid in cash)" is the cash paid to the remaining creditors after the 2% discount.
- **Loan to a partner** appears on the assets side of the memorandum balance sheet and is debited to that partner's capital before the realisation result.
