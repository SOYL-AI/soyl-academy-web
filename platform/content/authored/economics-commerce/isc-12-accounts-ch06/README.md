## Scope
All six syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml`:
- **t01** issue at par and at a premium under the Companies Act, 2013; Securities Premium and the Section 52 uses; entries in the **journal proper and the cash book** (bank column), with a mixed transfer-and-refund entry split between them; Equity Share Capital and Calls in Arrears ledger accounts.
- **t02** shares for consideration other than cash: to **promoters** (Incorporation Costs or Goodwill), to **underwriters** (commission capped at 5% of the issue price or the articles' rate, whichever is less, settled in shares at par or premium), to **vendors** for assets and for a business (goodwill or capital reserve as the balancing figure; number of shares = amount ÷ issue price).
- **t03** calls in arrears and calls in advance in **separate accounts**, with **interest as per the articles**, or Table F of Schedule I when they are silent (up to 10% p.a. on arrears from the due date to payment; up to 12% p.a. on advances from receipt to the due date), through Sundry Shareholders A/c and closed to the Statement of Profit and Loss.
- **t04** under-subscription with the 90% minimum subscription; over-subscription by rejection, full allotment and pro rata, with excess application money adjusted towards allotment (capital, then premium due) and only the surplus refunded.
- **t05** forfeiture of shares issued at par and at a premium (premium received or not; pro-rata allottees; partly called shares) and reissue at par, premium and discount, with capital reserve only on the shares reissued.
- **t06** disclosure under **Schedule III Part I**: Note on Share Capital (authorised, issued, subscribed and fully paid, subscribed but not fully paid less calls unpaid, add forfeited shares), Reserves and Surplus (securities premium, capital reserve), calls in advance under Other Current Liabilities.

Removed for ISC 2027 (bonus shares, rights shares, private placement, sweat equity shares and employee stock option schemes; reservations for small individual participants and minimum tradable lots) appear only as one "Not in the ISC 2027 syllabus" line in notes section 1. The Section 52 list in the chapter leaves out the bonus-issue use for the same reason.

## Verifier record (what the build computes and asserts)
- `acc.mjs` refuses any non-whole rupee and any non-whole share. Every issue is posted to its own `Books`: each entry balances; the capital parts of the instalments add to the face value; every instalment account closes at nil (or at the arrears); the 90% minimum subscription is checked; pro-rata holders' applied shares must be whole.
- `forfeit()` checks that the Share Forfeiture credit (called up + unpaid premium − unpaid) equals the money actually received on those shares less premium received, computed independently from the holder's application, adjustment and refund; calls in arrears left afterwards equal those of holders not forfeited (or already cleared).
- `reissue()` refuses a discount above the amount forfeited on the reissued shares and checks that the Share Forfeiture balance afterwards is exactly the forfeiture on shares not reissued.
- `interest()` refuses an interest figure that is not whole rupees; `receiveArrears()` and `payAdvanceInterest()` check that Sundry Shareholders closes; the year-end transfers leave both interest accounts at nil.
- `cashBookSplit()` splits mixed entries (transfer with refund) and checks that the cash-book balance equals the bank lines of the journal.
- `shareNote()` and `bsIII()` check that the note equals Share Capital − calls in arrears + Share Forfeiture from the ledger, and that both halves of the balance sheet total, with cash at bank taken from the Bank Account.

## Blueprint fit (ISC class default 1/2/3/5)
Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. Board questions on issue of shares are usually one practical of about 6–8 marks (a pro-rata issue with forfeiture and reissue in the journal, sometimes with a ledger account or the balance sheet). The default fits by splitting: 2-mark items are two or three entries or a note; 3-mark items are one stage of an issue, a cash book or a balance sheet extract; 5-mark items are set in parts (2 + 3, 3 + 2 or 2 + 2 + 1). Complete practicals are kept as 8-mark practice items outside the tests. A subject blueprint with a 6–8-mark practical would fit Accounts better.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Interest on calls in advance** is charged through Sundry Shareholders A/c and closed to the Statement of Profit and Loss at the year end; it is not described as payable "even without profits".
- **Securities premium on forfeiture**: debited only when the instalment carrying the premium was unpaid. Pro-rata defaulters are set either with no premium (Paraj, Shuddh Kalyan) or with the premium on application (Purvi), so the disputed case of premium partly covered by adjusted excess never arises.
- **Shares to promoters** use Incorporation Costs A/c (Goodwill A/c in one item, as the syllabus allows either).
- **Underwriting commission** is computed on the issue price of the shares underwritten, at the lower of 5% and the articles' rate.
- **Minimum subscription** is stated as 90% of a public issue.
- The running company's Board **waives interest** so its balance sheet stays a clean share-capital extract; interest is taught on Tilang and Hamsanandi.
