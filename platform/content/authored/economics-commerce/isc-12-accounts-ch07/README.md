## Scope
All five syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml` (no removed topics), with the notes of the ISC 2027 Accountancy syllabus (source `isc-2027-accounts`) applied:
- **t01** issue at par, at a premium and at a discount under the **Companies Act, 2013** (Section 2(30), Section 71 no voting rights, Section 53 bars only shares at a discount), payable in a lump sum or in instalments (application, allotment, first and final call), with refunds on over-subscription (13 items tagged).
- **t02** the six issue-redemption combinations; Discount on Issue (redeemable at par) vs **Loss on Issue** (discount + premium on redemption); Premium on Redemption as a liability; **all capital losses written off in the year they occur, first from Securities Premium, then from the Statement of Profit and Loss, lastly from Capital Reserve** (ISC note; Section 52) (21).
- **t03** collateral security by both methods (no entry with a note; Debenture Suspense, net nil); debentures to **promoters** (Incorporation Costs or Goodwill), **underwriters** (Underwriting Commission through the Underwriters A/c) and **vendors** of assets or a business (goodwill or capital reserve; number = amount ÷ issue price) (20).
- **t04** calls in arrears and calls in advance on debentures with interest on both; interest on debentures on face value with **TDS** (gross to Debenture Interest, net to holders, tax to TDS Payable, deposit, transfer to the Statement of Profit and Loss) (18).
- **t05** Schedule III Part I disclosure: long-term borrowings (nature of security, collateral less suspense, calls in arrears deducted), **current maturities of long-term borrowings** under short-term borrowings (amended Schedule III), Premium on Redemption under other long-term liabilities, calls in advance and TDS payable under other current liabilities, securities premium and capital reserve under Reserves and Surplus (13).

Items with several topics are counted under each. Redemption of debentures (DRR, DRI, methods) is ISC chapter 8: here redemption appears only as a **term of issue** (premium on redemption, loss on issue); notes section 1 says so in one line.

## Blueprint fit (ISC class default 1/2/3/5)
Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. Board debenture questions are usually one journal problem of 4–6 marks inside the 8-mark Issue and Redemption of Debentures unit. The default fits by splitting: 2-mark items are two or three entries, a rule or a short disclosure; 3-mark items are one issue from application to call, a write-off with its working, or a note; 5-mark items are set in parts (3 + 2 or 2 + 3). Two full practicals are kept as 8-mark practice items outside the tests.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Write-off order** follows the ISC 2027 note literally (Securities Premium, then the Statement of Profit and Loss, lastly Capital Reserve) and in the year of the loss. `s3_writeoff_cr` gives a limited amount "available in the Statement of Profit and Loss" so that capital reserve is reached; whether boards ever set such a cap is a reading of the note.
- **Discount vs Loss on Issue:** a discount alone (redeemable at par) is debited to Discount on Issue of Debentures; whenever a redemption premium exists, discount and premium go together to Loss on Issue of Debentures.
- **Promoters:** Incorporation Costs A/c is debited (Goodwill named as the alternative), following the ISC shares note for promoters.
- **Calls in arrears/advance on debentures:** interest rates are stated in each stem as terms of issue (10% on arrears, 12% on advance in the examples); interest on advance is treated as a finance cost.
- **TDS** at 10% on debenture interest; the tax on the March interest is shown as TDS payable under other current liabilities at the year end (WE13, `l5_disclosure`).
- **Current maturities** of debentures are shown under short-term borrowings (amended Schedule III); premium on redemption is kept wholly under other long-term liabilities.
