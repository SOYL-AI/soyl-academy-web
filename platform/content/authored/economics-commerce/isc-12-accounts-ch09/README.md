## Scope
All three syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml`, with the ISC 2027 exam note (company balance sheets follow Schedule III Part I):
- **t01** the Balance Sheet as per **Schedule III Part I** of the Companies Act, 2013 (Section 129): major heads and sub-heads in order, the operating-cycle and twelve-month tests for current items, classification of every common balance (28 items tagged).
- **t02** notes to accounts: **Share Capital** (authorised and issued for information; subscribed and fully paid; not fully paid less calls in arrears; add forfeited shares), **Reserves and Surplus** (surplus = opening + profit − transfers; a debit balance negative), **borrowings** (long-term, short-term), **Property, Plant and Equipment** (cost less accumulated depreciation), plus trade receivables, other current liabilities and inventories; the Balance Sheet **from a trial balance with adjustments** (36).
- **t03** the amendments named in the syllabus: **proposed dividend under contingent liabilities**, **Property, Plant and Equipment and Intangible Assets** (with Capital Work-in-Progress and Intangible Assets under Development), **current maturities of long-term borrowings under Short-term Borrowings**, **Securities Premium** (23).

Items with several topics are counted under each. **Removed for ISC 2027:** the Statement of Profit and Loss as per Schedule III Part II is not required for final accounts. It appears only as one "not in the ISC 2027 syllabus" line in notes section 1 and as a row of the law table; wherever a profit is needed it is either given or found in one working from a given profit and the adjustments (trial balances show "Profit for the year before the adjustments below").

## Verifier record (what the build computes and asserts)
- **One classification table** (`CLASSIFY`, amended Schedule III Part I order) feeds every classification key, every option built with `where()`/`subOf()`, every Balance Sheet and every note, so an item cannot be placed two ways; unlisted items throw.
- `shareNote()` asserts subscribed + forfeited shares ≤ issued ≤ authorised, arrears only on shares not fully paid, forfeited amount below face value, and that the note adds up. `bs()` asserts every sub-head total (only Reserves and Surplus may be negative), every note total, and that **Equity and Liabilities = Assets** (or that an extract holds one part only). `trialBalance()` asserts Dr = Cr. `finalAccounts()` checks the trial balance's share capital, calls in arrears and forfeiture against the share note, links accumulated depreciation to its asset, refuses a current maturity or depreciation on a line not in the trial balance, posts each adjustment to the profit working and to one balance sheet line, and then relies on `bs()`: any slip in an adjustment unbalances the Balance Sheet and stops the build. ## Originality
Every company, place-and-trade pairing and figure is SOYL's own and labelled "imagined"; the companies are named after Indian waterfalls (Thoseghar, Kunchikal, Dudhsagar, Athirapally, Chitrakote and others), a theme not used in the CBSE 12 Accountancy chapters or ISC ch01–ch07. Names were checked word by word against those sources and the sibling ISC ch08; two place words shared with ch08 (Kuntala, Pochera) were renamed. Every stem names its own company or balance, including match, true-false, fill-blank and assertion-reason lead-ins. No textbook illustration or exercise and no CISCE specimen or past-paper item was used or reconstructed; the Act and Schedule III are paraphrased.

## Blueprint fit (ISC class default 1/2/3/5)
Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. The board question on this unit is usually one Balance Sheet practical of about 7 marks. The default fits by splitting it: 2-mark items are four classifications, one short note or a rule with an example; 3-mark items are one note with its working (share capital, reserves, borrowings, PPE) or six classifications; 5-mark items are a small Balance Sheet with notes, or a trial balance with a few adjustments, set in parts. Two full practicals are kept as 8-mark practice items outside the tests.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Profit in the trial balance:** following the syllabus (Part II not required), trial balances are given after the profit has been found and show "Profit for the year before the adjustments below"; the profit for the year is then worked out in one working. Whether the board phrases it this way or simply states the net profit is a reading of the syllabus note.
- **Proposed dividend** is shown under *Contingent Liabilities and Commitments (to the extent not provided for)*, as the syllabus topic states; it is never provided for or deducted from the surplus.
- **Notes given:** Share Capital, Reserves and Surplus, Long-term and Short-term Borrowings and PPE always get a note (the notes the syllabus names); other sub-heads get one when they have more than one item, a deduction or a working.
- **Current maturities** of debentures and term loans go to Short-term Borrowings; interest accrued on debentures to Other Current Liabilities; premium on redemption to Other Long-term Liabilities.
- **Forfeited shares** not reissued are added in the Share Capital note; calls in advance are an Other Current Liability.
- **Operating cycle:** `s3_op_cycle` treats an amount owed to farmers payable in thirteen months, within a sixteen-month cycle, as a current liability (Trade Payables), and aged rice to be sold in fifteen months as inventory.
