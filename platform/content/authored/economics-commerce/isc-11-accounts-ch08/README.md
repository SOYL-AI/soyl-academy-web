## Scope
All four syllabus topics of the ISC 2028 Class XI Accounts chapter (published weight: Bank Reconciliation Statement 10 marks of 80):
- **t01** need for reconciliation and the causes of difference: timing differences (cheques issued not presented, cheques deposited not credited), items first in the pass book (bank charges, interest on overdraft, interest credited, direct deposits, payments on standing instruction, amounts collected by the bank, dishonoured cheques) and errors in either book — 19 items.
- **t02** a statement from a given cash book or pass book balance, favourable or an overdraft, by the add and less method, with the two-column (plus and minus) form as an option — 24 items plus one shared with t01.
- **t03** a statement from extracts of the cash book and the pass book: matching by amount and nature, the unmatched items, items of the previous period (cleared, or still open) and extracts with overdrafts — 17 items.
- **t04** the amended (adjusted) cash book followed by a statement from its balance, including a correction of a cash book error and an opening overdraft — 18 items.
- Removed topics: none. GST: no transaction anywhere; the tips say GST is not examined.

## Verifier record (what the build computes and asserts)
`src/recon.mjs` models every difference as an item with a `gap` (pass book minus cash book), so no add or deduct rule is typed twice: the treatment tables, rule grids, slides and solutions are generated from the nine causes.
- **Both directions:** every scenario (`scn()` in `fig.mjs`) builds the statement from the cash book and from the pass book and asserts that each reaches the other book's balance exactly (through `acc.mjs brs()` and again on the printed rows).
- **Overdraft layout:** an overdraft start prints the reversed layout (an item that would lower a favourable balance is added); `statement()` asserts that the printed arithmetic reaches the result, and the self-checks pin the layout.
- **Two-column form:** `twoColumn()` asserts plus total less minus total equals the same result.
- **Extracts:** `twoBooks()` builds both books from a list of transactions and finds both closing balances independently; every unmatched transaction must carry a cause whose gap equals its effect, items of the previous period must close (or stay open) exactly, and the opening difference must equal the opening items. The cash book extract is a two-sided account with equal totals; the pass book extract has a running balance whose last figure equals the independent total.
- **Amended cash book:** posted with `ledger()` (c/d, b/d, equal totals), the new balance is asserted against balance plus the fixes; the statement from it is asserted to reach the pass book, and the pass book start (with the remaining items) is asserted to reach the amended balance.
- Key figures are pinned to hand-worked values (Vasudha Pottery Works pass book ₹56,910; Jasmeet Dye House cash book overdraft ₹17,950; Ravine Textiles pass book overdraft ₹12,100; Ketan Printers amended balance ₹39,035 and pass book ₹42,935; Brightline Hardware amended overdraft ₹18,600; Lotus Bindery cash book ₹26,600 and pass book ₹29,030; Mirage Auto Parts overdrafts ₹24,200 and ₹21,550). Extra gates in `build.mjs`: TTS-safe narration; near-duplicate stems in the bank (two pairs at 66–67%, never together in a test); test disjointness, no deck-check item in a test, and marks (20 and 40); at most two slides in a row without a visual.

## Flagged treatments (a reviewer should double-check)
- **Statement layout:** the ISC style Add and Less groups with subtotals; an overdraft start uses the reversed layout (items that deepen the overdraft are added). The CBSE-style one-line formulae are not used.
- **Dishonoured cheque:** treated as a bank item (pass book first); the cash book credits the bank column. The firm's earlier receipt is assumed to be in the cash book, so a dishonoured cheque the firm already knew of is not tested.
- **Cheque entered in the cash book for the wrong amount:** treated as an error of the cash book, corrected through the amended cash book (debit if the receipt was understated, credit if a payment was understated). Errors by the bank are explained in the notes and decks but only one assertion mentions them; no item asks the student to prepare a statement with a bank error.
- **Items of the previous period:** shown as opening statement items (cheques issued or deposited in the earlier month); a cleared item drops out and an open item stays. Matching is by amount and nature, not by date.
- **Pass book layout:** withdrawals, deposits and a running balance (Cr favourable, Dr overdraft). Real bank statements vary.
- **Amended cash book layout:** a two-sided bank column account (opening balance on the debit side if favourable, on the credit side if an overdraft) without the cash and discount columns; the closing line is "Balance c/d".
- **Assertion-reason options** follow the CISCE Class XI Accountancy specimen wording and order: (d) is "Both Assertion and Reason are false".
