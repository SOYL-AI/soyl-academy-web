## Scope
All six syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml`:
- **t01** new ratio and gaining ratio: nothing agreed (old ratio continues), fractions of the firm acquired, outgoing share taken in a given ratio, a stated new ratio (including a continuing partner who **sacrifices**); sacrificing vs gaining ratio; Sections 32 and 42 of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932.
- **t02** goodwill under Accounting Standard 26 (gaining partners debited; a sacrificing continuing partner credited too), book goodwill written off among all partners, **hidden goodwill** from an agreed settlement; Revaluation Account shared by all partners in the old ratio; general reserve, profit and loss balances, advertisement suspense, workmen compensation reserve (claim below and above the reserve) and investment fluctuation reserve (fall below and above the reserve).
- **t03** deceased partner's share of profit till death on the **time basis** (last year or an average) and the **turnover basis**, recorded through **Profit and Loss Suspense** or the **gaining partners**; interest on capital and drawings to the date of death.
- **t04** continuing partners' capitals readjusted to an agreed total, and **cash brought in** to pay the outgoing partner while keeping a minimum bank balance, capitals ending in the new ratio.
- **t05** amount due; payment by bank, loan or both; Section 37; the **loan account** with equal instalments and interest on the balance, both at year ends (accrued and due) and mid-year (**accrued but not due** at 31 March); the deceased partner's capital account, the **executor's account** and the executor's loan.
- **t06** the **Balance Sheet in horizontal form** after retirement or death (loan or executor's loan; Profit and Loss Suspense among the assets; reserves removed).

Removed for ISC 2027 (memorandum revaluation account; joint life policy and individual life policies) appear only as one "not in the ISC 2027 syllabus" line in notes section 1.

## Verifier record (what the build computes and asserts)
- `acc.mjs` works in exact fractions. `retire()` checks that old and new shares each add to 1 and that gains less sacrifices equal the outgoing share; a continuing partner who loses is refused unless the item allows it. Its `goodwill()` checks that debits equal credits and allocates each gainer's debit over the credited partners in whole rupees (for the columnar accounts). Every rupee split must be whole; rates of profit on sales must be whole percentages.
- `journal()` throws unless each entry balances; `tAccount()`, `revaluation()` and `capitalAccounts()` throw unless both sides total (no capital account may close in debit; the outgoing partner's must close at nil); `balanceSheet()` throws unless assets equal liabilities. `loanAccount()` builds every accounting year from the instalment and month arithmetic (interest whole, each year balancing, accrued interest carried down, loan fully repaid).
- `solveRetirement()` derives the decks' running example (Gulmohar Marble Polishers), two long-5 items and the three 8-mark practicals from the old balance sheet and the terms (revaluation, reserves with the claim/fall rules, book goodwill, stated or hidden goodwill, profit till death, payment and loan or executor's loan, capital adjustment to a total or by cash brought in to pay out and keep a minimum balance). The new balance sheet is built independently from the revised lines, so its balancing cross-checks the capital accounts; the bank working is re-added and may not go negative, and cash brought in must reach the minimum balance exactly.
- Key figures of every computed item and worked example (ratios, gains and sacrifices, goodwill splits, hidden goodwill, revaluation results, profit till death, loan payments and year-end balances, amounts due, closing capitals, balance-sheet totals, bank) are pinned to hand-checked values; any drift throws. ## Blueprint fit (ISC class default 1/2/3/5)
Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. The board's partnership questions are larger practicals (Revaluation Account, capital accounts and a Balance Sheet together). The default fits by splitting: 2-mark items are a ratio working, one or two entries or a short computation; 3-mark items are one account (Revaluation, loan, capital + executor's) or a set of entries; 5-mark items are set in parts (2 + 3 or 3 + 2). Complete practicals are kept as 8-mark practice items outside the tests. A subject blueprint with 6- and 8-mark practicals would fit Accounts better.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Hidden goodwill on retirement** (`m_hidden`, `n_hidden_gw`, `s2_hidden`, WE4, `l8_hidden_cash`): settlement − capital after all other adjustments = the outgoing partner's share; ÷ his profit share = firm's goodwill; charged to the gainers in the gaining ratio.
- **Continuing partner who sacrifices** (`m_sacrifice_cont`, `s3_ratio_sacr`, WE3): credited for goodwill together with the outgoing partner; the gainer pays both in one entry.
- **Loan accrued but not due** (`m_loan_interest`, `tf_loan_interest`, `s3_loan_mid`, WE9): interest for the months run is credited to the loan account on 31 March and carried down; on the instalment date the next interest is credited and the instalment plus twelve months' interest is paid.
- **Profit and Loss Suspense** is shown on the assets side of a balance sheet drawn up on the date of death (`tf_suspense`, `s3_bs`, `l8_death`); the death practical takes the other lines at their 31 March balances, as its stem says.
- **Workmen compensation claim** and **outstanding rent** brought in on retirement appear among the liabilities of the new balance sheet (WE7).
- **A retired partner's liability to third parties until public notice** is stated in notes section 1 (Section 32).
