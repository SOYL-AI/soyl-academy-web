## Scope
All three syllabus topics (2026-27): **t01** meaning, objectives, cash and cash equivalents (Accounting Standard 3; three-month test from acquisition; equity shares excluded), cash flows, what is not a cash flow (movements within cash and cash equivalents, non-cash transactions) (14 items) · **t02** classification into operating, investing and financing activities, including interest and dividends for non-financial and financial enterprises and income tax paid (15) · **t03** preparation by the **indirect method**: net profit before tax rebuilt from the surplus, reserves, dividends and tax provision; non-cash and non-operating adjustments; working-capital changes; tax paid; working accounts for assets (at cost with accumulated depreciation, and at written-down value), investments and provision for tax; investing and financing sections; the cash check (32) · 4 items span two topics. The **direct method is mentioned only** (one deck slide, notes section 5, one MCQ distractor and a flashcard). **Extraordinary items** (removed for 2026-27) appear only as one "not in the syllabus" line in notes section 5 and one flashcard; every statement starts from "net profit before tax". Redemption of debentures (removed) is not used: loans are repaid, debentures are only issued.

**Conventions used everywhere** (notes sections 4–7, `acc.mjs`): non-financial company — interest and dividend received are investing, interest and dividend paid are financing; financial company — interest received, dividend received and interest paid are operating; income tax paid is operating; short-term bank borrowings and cash credit are financing; tax **paid** (from the provision for tax account) is deducted, the provision is added back through net profit before tax; the full sale price goes under investing and only the profit or loss is adjusted in operating; interest is worked on the balance and period outstanding.

## Verifier record (what the build computes and asserts)

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Short-term borrowings** (bank loan, cash credit) are treated as financing activities (the usual school treatment); some texts treat a bank overdraft repayable on demand as part of cash and cash equivalents.
- **Dividends paid** during 2025-26 are the final dividend for 2024-25 and any interim dividend (proposed dividends are not liabilities under the current Schedule III); both are added back in rebuilding net profit before tax and shown under financing.
- **Interest** on debentures and loans is assumed paid in full when due, so finance cost added back = interest paid; there is no accrued or outstanding interest in any problem.
- The companies are not financial enterprises except where stated (Kali Sindh Finance, Mutha Housing Finance).
- Statement tables, ledgers and the SVG should be checked at 360 px on staging (four-column ledgers are wide).
