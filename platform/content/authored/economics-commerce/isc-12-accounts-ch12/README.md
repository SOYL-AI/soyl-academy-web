## Scope
All five syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml`, with exactly the ratios the ISC list names:
- **t01** liquidity: current ratio; quick (liquid, acid-test) ratio, with current assets and liabilities taken from the Schedule III heads (bills receivable in trade receivables, bank overdraft as a short-term borrowing) (19 items).
- **t02** solvency: debt to equity, proprietary, **debt to total assets**, interest coverage, with interpretation (16).
- **t03** activity: trade receivables, trade payables, inventory and working capital turnover, using averages and credit figures; cost of revenue from operations and average inventory found from given data, including gross profit on cost (21).
- **t04** profitability: gross profit, net profit, operating and operating profit ratios (operating separated from non-operating items), **earnings per share**, **price-earnings ratio**, return on investment with capital employed (27).
- **t05** effects of transactions on the **current, quick and debt-equity ratios only** (12).

Items with several topics are counted under each. Missing-figure problems run through every family (liquidity, inventory, profitability, earnings per share and market price). **Removed for ISC 2027** (effect-of-transaction problems on any other ratio) appears only as one "not in the ISC 2027 syllabus" line in notes section 5. Meaning and objectives of ratio analysis are covered briefly (deck slides 2–3, notes section 1) as the frame for the four groups; limitations of ratio analysis are not in this chapter's topic list and are not taught.

**Definitions used everywhere** (notes section 2, `acc.mjs`): current assets and current liabilities by the Schedule III heads; quick assets = current assets − inventories − prepaid expenses; debt = long-term borrowings + long-term provisions; equity = shareholders' funds; total assets = non-current + current assets; working capital = current assets − current liabilities at the year end; capital employed = shareholders' funds + non-current liabilities = non-current assets + working capital = total assets − current liabilities (all three asserted equal on every balance sheet); cost of revenue from operations = opening inventory + net purchases + direct expenses − closing inventory (= revenue − gross profit); averages = (opening + closing) ÷ 2; operating expenses exclude finance costs and non-operating items; profit before interest and tax = operating profit + non-operating income − non-operating expenses; net profit ratio on profit after tax; earnings per share = (profit after tax − preference dividend) ÷ number of equity shares; price-earnings = market price per share ÷ earnings per share. **Rounding:** two decimal places, half up, trailing zeros dropped; earnings per share in rupees and paise; a rounded result is written with "≈" in workings.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Debt** is long-term borrowings + long-term provisions in both debt to equity and **debt to total assets** (the syllabus names "debt to total assets"; some books use total outside liabilities, or the inverse "total assets to debt"). One definition is used throughout and stated in notes section 2.
- **Capital employed** is not reduced by non-current (non-trade) investments; interest received on them is treated as non-operating income in the profit before interest and tax.
- **Working capital turnover** uses year-end working capital; inventory, receivables and payables turnover use simple averages. Credit figures are used where given.
- **Net profit ratio** is on profit after tax (the question says so where it matters).
- **Effects:** a creditor for machinery is treated as a current liability (s3_eff_all); preference shares redeemed at par out of profits reduce shareholders' funds by the amount paid, the capital redemption reserve transfer staying inside equity (l5_de_effects).
