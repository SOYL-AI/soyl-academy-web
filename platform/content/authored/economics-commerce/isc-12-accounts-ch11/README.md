## Scope
All five syllabus topics of `platform/catalog/cisce/isc-12/accounts/syllabus.yaml`, indirect method under AS 3 (revised) only:
- **t01** meaning and importance; cash and cash equivalents (the three-month test from the date of **acquisition**; an old bond close to maturity fails); what is not a cash flow; classification into operating, investing and financing activities, including financial versus non-financial enterprises (26 items).
- **t02** operating activities by the indirect method: net profit before tax as a working note, non-cash and non-operating adjustments, working-capital changes, tax paid (16).
- **t03** investing activities: purchase and sale of fixed assets (at cost with accumulated depreciation, and at written-down value), intangibles, investments, loans given, interest and dividend received (16).
- **t04** financing activities: issue of equity shares with premium, **share issue expenses**, redemption of debentures and **preference shares at a premium** (premium written off against securities premium), loans raised and repaid, interest worked period by period, dividends paid (15).
- **t05** adjustments: provision for tax (tax paid or provision as the balancing figure), **previous year’s proposed dividend as an outflow and the current year’s proposed dividend as having no effect**, interim dividend, depreciation and sale of assets, amortisation (goodwill, patents), **provision for doubtful debts** (increase added back with gross receivables; shown to equal the net-receivables method); complete statements (22).

Items with several topics are counted under each. **Removed for ISC 2027** (extraordinary items and refund of tax; transactions relating to capital reserve; the adjusted Profit and Loss Account method; the direct method) appear only as one "not in the ISC 2027 syllabus" line in notes section 4. A capital redemption reserve (a different reserve, created on redeeming preference shares) appears in two items as a transfer out of profit.

## Blueprint fit (ISC class default 1/2/3/5)
Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. The board question on this unit is usually one complete statement of about 8 marks. The default fits by splitting it: 2-mark items are one working (net profit before tax, a tax or depreciation account, cash equivalents) or four classifications; 3-mark items are one section with its working, or a working account with its treatment; 5-mark items are a section with two working accounts, or a small complete statement in parts (working notes 2, statement 3). Three full practicals are kept as 8-mark practice items outside the tests.

## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Dividends:** following the syllabus outcome, the previous year’s proposed dividend paid this year is added back in the profit working and shown as a financing outflow; the current year’s proposed dividend (a contingent liability) is neither added nor shown. A preference dividend for the previous year paid this year is treated the same way.
- **Provision for doubtful debts:** receivables are shown gross with the provision deducted; the increase (decrease) in the provision is added back (deducted) as a non-cash item before working capital, and the change in gross receivables goes to working capital. The deck, `m_pdd` and `s3_pdd_amort` show that this equals the net-receivables method.
- **Securities premium:** share issue expenses and premiums on redemption are written off against securities premium, so the premium received on a new issue is the balancing figure of that account; issue expenses and redemption premiums are financing outflows.
- **Capital redemption reserve** is treated as a transfer out of profit (added back in the profit working, no cash flow). It is not the "capital reserve" removed from the syllabus.
- **Cash equivalents:** the three-month test is applied from the date of acquisition (an old debenture now 40 or 50 days from maturity is excluded).
