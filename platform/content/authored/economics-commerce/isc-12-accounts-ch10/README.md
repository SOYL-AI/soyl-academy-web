## Verifier record (what the build computes and asserts)
- **Statements:** `plStatement()` computes total income, total expenses, profit before tax, tax in whole rupees (refuses a fractional rupee) and profit after tax for both columns and re-adds every relation; `bsStatement()` computes head and part totals and refuses a balance sheet whose two parts differ.
- **Comparative:** `comparative()` computes every absolute change and every percentage change in **integer hundredths** (no floating-point rounding), cross-checks each against the long division, asserts that the changes of every total re-add through the same relations and that both balance sheet totals change by the same amount and percentage; head-level changes are computed on the head totals.
- **Common-size:** `commonSize()` asserts the base line is 100.00; in a balance sheet each part of each column totals exactly 100.00 after the stated adjustment (the line whose rounding error is largest in the direction of the excess moves by 0.01 and is marked †, with a generated note) and no line ends more than 0.01 from its exact value; in profit and loss the rounded lines must still re-add (total income, total expenses, profit before tax, profit after tax), or the build throws.
- **Rounding rule** (stated in notes, decks and every model answer that rounds): two decimals, a half rounded away from zero; checked on true halves (Rohida 15.625 → 15.63, Mulher 9.375 → 9.38).
- Both SVGs' geometry is computed from the statements (bar lengths are the percentage changes; stacked segments add to total income; head columns add to 100.00, asserted).
- **Pinned results** (independently hand-checked): Shivneri total income ₹51,00,000 / ₹60,90,000, total expenses ₹41,00,000 / ₹49,50,000, profit after tax ₹7,00,000 / ₹7,98,000, changes 20.00, (10.00), 19.41, 24.29, 10.00, 50.00, 20.00, 5.00, 20.73, 14.00, 14.00; balance sheet ₹42,00,000 / ₹48,40,000 (15.24; reserves 33.33; cash (6.67)); heads 26.92, (20.00), 23.33, 15.33, 15.00; Jaigarh–Kumbhalgarh 25.00, 35.42, 32.81, (6.25); Panhala shares 2.00/1.00, 102.00/101.00, 55.00/58.00, 79.00/80.00, 23.00/21.00, 6.90/6.30, 16.10/14.70, balance sheet † on non-current investments (4.54), heads 60.00/60.61, 20.00/21.21, 20.00/18.18, 65.00/68.18, 35.00/31.82; Sindhudurg–Vijaydurg 80.00/81.00, 15.00/14.25; and every bank item (for example Torna total expenses ₹22,50,000 (28.57), profit 0.00; Rajgad ₹19,00,000 (18.75); Daulatabad profit after tax ₹10,71,000 (27.50), materials 29.55, finance (16.67); Gingee ₹46,00,000 (15.00), current assets 9.23; Vishalgad profit before tax ₹11,60,000 (45.00); Asirgarh 11.90 → 10.50 with rupee profit +5.88; Raisen 16.00 → 15.60; Chittorgarh share capital 41.66 †, shareholders' funds 65.00 → 60.00; Chapora–Terekhol 15.00 each; Korigad 50.00, 26.00, 12.50 → 10.50; Janjira ₹75,00,000 (25.00); Kangla 26.00, 14.00 → 14.70).

Accounts has no subject blueprint, so the tests use the ISC 12 defaults. The board weightage for this unit is only 4 marks (usually one short statement or a theory question), so the default pattern over-represents the unit but fits its shape: 2-mark items are two changes, two shares or two theory points; 3-mark items are one short statement (both years completed 1, each set of figures 1) or an interpretation; 5-mark items are a full statement with observations in parts (3 + 2). ## For a reviewer to double-check
- **Rounding:** halves are rounded away from zero; common-size balance sheet parts are forced to 100.00 by moving one line by 0.01 (†, explained in the answer). Some textbooks leave a part at 99.99 or 100.01; the answer explains the adjustment, so either is defensible in an exam, but SOYL's keys show the adjusted form. Head shares used in comments are each head total ÷ the part total, rounded on their own.
- **Percentage change on a nil base** is shown as a dash; **on a total** it is worked on the total, never averaged.
- **Common-size profit and loss** uses revenue from operations (not total income) as 100, so total income can exceed 100.
- **Inter-firm comparative statements** take the first firm as the base (difference = other firm − base firm, percentage on the base firm).
- **Tax** is given as a whole-number rate of profit before tax in every statement; the Statement of Profit and Loss has no exceptional or extraordinary items.
