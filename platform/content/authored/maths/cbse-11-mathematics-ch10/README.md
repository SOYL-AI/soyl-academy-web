## Scope (2026-27 syllabus, 041)
- **t01** Sections of a cone; circle: the four sections by the angle `beta` between the plane and the axis against the semi-vertical angle `alpha` (and the degenerate sections through the vertex), the focus-directrix idea and `e`, the circle in standard form, the general form with centre `(-g, -f)` and `r^2 = g^2 + f^2 - c` (including the point-circle and no-circle cases), a circle on a diameter, through three points, position of a point, chords cut on an axis, touching an axis.
- **t02** Parabola: the four standard forms with vertex at the origin, focus, directrix, axis, latus rectum `4a` and its ends, focal distance, equation from the focus, directrix or a point, a reflector and a cable as applications.
- **t03** Ellipse and hyperbola in standard forms: foci, vertices, major/minor and transverse/conjugate axes, eccentricity, latus rectum `2b^2/a`, asymptotes, equations from foci, vertices, eccentricity or two points, the focal properties (constant sum / constant difference), and the comparison of the four conics.
- No topic in this chapter is `board_assessed: false`; all three are board assessed. The subject-level removed topics do not appear. Not covered (outside the syllabus list): tangents and normals, parametric forms, the rectangular hyperbola, translated (non-central) conics, and the general second-degree equation.
