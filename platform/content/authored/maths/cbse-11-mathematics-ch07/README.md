## Scope (2026-27 syllabus, 041)
- **t01** Binomial theorem for positive integral indices; Pascal's triangle (board assessed): the triangle and its history (India, Persia, China, France), statement of the theorem, the counting proof (one letter from each bracket), features (`n + 1` terms, symmetry, sum `2^n`, alternating sum, `(a + b)^n ± (a - b)^n`), negative second term and fractions, numerical values (`52^4`, `99^3`, `1004^3`), small-`x` estimates, remainders and divisibility.
- **t02** General and middle terms (**`board_assessed: false`, formative only**): `T(r+1) = C(n, r) a^(n-r) b^r`, a particular term, the term independent of `x`, middle terms (even and odd `n`), greatest binomial coefficient, equal binomial coefficients. Taught but kept light: deck 2 and notes sections 5-7 are marked formative, t02 is 21 of the 71 topic tags on the questions, and the short chapter test has no t02 item (the long test has three, each as one side of an internal choice).
- The chapter has no `removed_topics`. Mathematical induction (formative only for the subject) appears in one note sentence only (the alternative proof exists, the counting proof is the one taught). The binomial theorem for a fractional or negative index is outside the syllabus list and is not covered.
