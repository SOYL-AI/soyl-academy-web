## Scope (catalog/cisce/icse-10/mathematics/syllabus.yaml)
- **t01** Right circular cylinder: curved and total surface area, volume, open tanks, hollow cylinders (pipes).
- **t02** Right circular cone: slant height, curved and total surface area, volume, finding r, l or h from a given area.
- **t03** Sphere, hemisphere (solid and bowl) and spherical shells.
- **t04** Cost problems (area x rate, litres x rate), unit conversion, inner and outer volume, mass from density.
- **t05** Melting and recasting: equal volumes, one solid into many, rod into wire, solid submerged in water.
- **t06** Combinations: cylinder, cone and hemisphere joined; only outside faces counted.
- Removed for ICSE 2027: the frustum of a cone (one note in the cone notes section).
