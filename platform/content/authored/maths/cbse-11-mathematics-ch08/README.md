## Scope (2026-27 syllabus, 041)
- **t01** Sequences and series; arithmetic progression revision: sequences by formula and by recursive rule, partial sums, `a_n = S_n - S_(n-1)`, the AP general term and sum (recap level), three numbers in AP, number of terms and sums of finite APs.
- **t02** Geometric progression: general term `a r^(n-1)`, sum of `n` terms (`r < 1` and `r > 1` forms, `r = 1`), three numbers in GP, a ratio from two terms (both signs when the power is even), the infinite GP and `S = a/(1 - r)` for `|r| < 1` (with the remainder `a r^n/(1 - r)`), recurring decimals as fractions.
- **t03** Arithmetic and geometric means; the AM–GM relation: AM of two numbers, `n` AMs and `n` GMs inserted between two numbers (both sets of GMs when the ratio can be negative), `A >= G` for two positive numbers with the proof and the equality case `a = b`, greatest product for a fixed sum and least sum for a fixed product.
- **t04** Sums of `n`, `n^2` and `n^3` is `board_assessed: false` (formative only). It is kept light: one concept slide and one worked example in Part 1, one note section marked "formative only", one flashcard, and three practice items (`m19`, `ar6`, `n07`) that are not placed in either chapter test.
- The subject-level removed topics (Mathematical Induction beyond formative use, Mathematical Reasoning) do not appear in this chapter; the sum formulas are quoted and checked by computation, not proved by induction. The sum of the first `n` squares and cubes are used only through the standard formulas. Arithmetic–geometric progressions, harmonic progressions and the harmonic mean are outside the syllabus list and are not covered.
