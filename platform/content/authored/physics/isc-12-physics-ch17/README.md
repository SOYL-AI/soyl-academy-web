## Scope
- Follows `catalog/cisce/isc-12/physics/syllabus.yaml` ch17: t01 wavefronts (spherical, cylindrical, plane, with I ∝ 1/r², 1/r, constant) and Huygens’ principle (forward envelope of wavelets of radius vt); t02 the laws of reflection and refraction proved with Huygens’ construction (sin i / sin r = v₁/v₂ = n₂/n₁), speed, wavelength and frequency in a medium, denser to rarer and the wavelet that outgrows AC (no refracted wave); t03 superposition, φ = 2πΔ/λ, conditions for constructive and destructive interference, coherent and incoherent sources, conditions for a sustained pattern, I_max/I_min from the amplitudes; t04 Young’s double slit: Δ = d sin θ ≈ yd/D, bright and dark fringe positions, β = λD/d (derived), angular fringe width λ/d, effect of λ, d, D and the medium, white light; t05 the intensity distribution (I = I₁ + I₂ + 2√(I₁I₂) cos φ and I = 4I₀ cos²(φ/2) **quoted, not derived**), its sketch, the average 2I₀ and the contrast with unequal slits; t06 Fraunhofer single-slit diffraction at the elementary level: minima a sin θ = nλ by pairing halves of the slit, secondary maxima near (n + ½)λ, faint (first under 1/20 of the centre); t07 angular (2λ/a) and linear (2λD/a) width of the central maximum.
- **Removed in ISC and not examined:** refraction through a prism and a lens with Huygens’ theory; the mathematical deduction of interference from the equations of two progressive waves (the intensity results are stated, and a deck slide says so); single-slit diffraction beyond the elementary qualitative treatment (no intensity formula is examined; the computed plots are for reading only).
- **Not in this chapter:** polarisation (not in the ISC syllabus entry for ch17), Fresnel distance, thin-film and Lloyd’s-mirror arrangements, fringe shift by a thin sheet.
- Line-source items place the observer close to a long tube or wire (10–40 cm from a 1.5–3 m source), where the cylindrical-wavefront model holds.
