## Scope
- Follows `catalog/cisce/isc-12/physics/syllabus.yaml` ch15: t01 refraction at a single spherical surface, n₂/v − n₁/u = (n₂ − n₁)/R, **derived only for a convex surface facing the rarer medium with a real image** (the syllabus’s removed list), then used with signs for other cases (a bead close to a rod end, a fish in a bowl, a hemispherical paperweight, a glass marble), the two focal lengths of a surface and m = n₁v/(n₂u); t02 the lens maker’s formula and thin lens formula derived for a biconvex lens from the two surfaces, extended to biconcave, plano-convex and equiconvex lenses and to a lens in a liquid (n replaced by n/n_m, including a liquid denser than the glass and an air lens in glass); t03 magnification m = v/u and power P = 1/f in dioptres with signs, the 1/v–1/u graph, the displacement method; t04 thin lenses in contact, 1/F = 1/f₁ + 1/f₂ and P = P₁ + P₂ (derived); t05 separated lenses (u₂ = v₁ − d, virtual objects, m = m₁m₂) and a lens with a separate plane, concave or convex mirror (auto-collimation, the lens–plane-mirror example, images falling back on the object).
- **Removed in ISC and not examined:** silvered lenses (the decks say so; every mirror here is separate from the lens); derivations of the surface formula for other cases.
- Sign convention: New Cartesian throughout (light from left to right; real object u < 0; R positive when the centre of curvature is on the outgoing side; converging lens f > 0; on a return pass after a mirror, distances are measured along the returning light).
- Not examined here: prisms (ch14) and optical instruments (ch16); the separated-lens items avoid instrument set-ups and names.
