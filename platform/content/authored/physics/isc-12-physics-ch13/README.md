## Scope
- Follows `catalog/cisce/isc-12/physics/syllabus.yaml` ch13: t01 reflection by spherical mirrors (pole, centre and radius of curvature, principal focus, paraxial rays), the proof of R = 2f, the New Cartesian sign convention, the derivation of the mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f for a concave mirror forming a real image, magnification m = h′/h = −v/u, and numericals for concave and convex mirrors; t02 refraction at a plane surface, the laws of refraction and Snell’s law, n = c/v, relative refractive index ₁n₂ = n₂/n₁ = v₁/v₂, the proof of ₁n₂ × ₂n₃ × ₃n₁ = 1 for parallel media, rays through parallel slabs (emergent ray parallel, lateral shift t sin(i − r)/cos r); t03 real and apparent depth (n = real/apparent for near-normal viewing), the apparent shift t(1 − 1/n) through a slab or liquid, several layers (Σ t/n), the travelling-microscope method, an object in air seen from under water; t04 total internal reflection, the critical angle (sin C = 1/n, or n₂/n₁ between two media), total reflecting prisms (45°–90°–45° turning light through 90° and 180°; 30°–60°–90° with incidences of 60° (total for n > 1.155) and 30° (needing n > 2); an equilateral prism turning a ray through 60°), and optical fibres (core and cladding, guiding by repeated TIR, the largest entry angle, uses).
- The syllabus lists no removed topics for this chapter. Sign convention: New Cartesian throughout (light drawn from left to right; real object u < 0, concave f < 0, convex f > 0, real image v < 0).
- Not examined here because they belong to other chapters: refraction through a prism, deviation and dispersion (ch14), refraction at spherical surfaces and lenses (ch15), optical instruments (ch16). Prisms appear only as total reflectors.
