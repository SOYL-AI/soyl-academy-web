## Scope
- **t01** work done by a constant force as the scalar product, W = F·d = F d cos θ, sign of the work, the component form of the scalar product, work by a variable force as the area under the F–x graph and as an integral. **t02** kinetic energy (K = ½mv² = p²/2m) and the work–energy theorem (derived for a constant force and sketched for a variable force). **t03** conservative and non-conservative forces, potential energy (mgh, reference level, F = −dU/dx), the spring force and ½kx², conservation of mechanical energy (roller-coaster track, block and spring), the energy ledger with friction (ΔK + ΔU = work of friction). **t04** power (average, instantaneous, P = F·v, units, the kilowatt hour), collisions: elastic, inelastic and perfectly inelastic in one dimension (target at rest closed forms, perfectly inelastic with a ballistic pendulum) and a simple two-dimensional treatment (momentum components; equal masses, elastic, target at rest: paths at right angles).
- **Left out on purpose, as the Lead's scope ruling directs (not named in the syllabus topics and uncertain in the rationalised book):** vertical circular motion; the equivalence of mass and energy and the other forms of energy (heat, chemical, nuclear); the coefficient of restitution. Relative velocity is not used in any form: the elastic-collision derivation divides the energy equation by the momentum equation and does not call the result a relative velocity.
- Data and constants stated in the stems: g = 9.8 m s⁻²; cos 30° = 0.866 and cos 37° = 0.80 (stated where used); 1 kW h = 3.6 × 10⁶ J; 1 hp = 746 W appears only in the units table. No NCERT, exemplar or past-paper text is used; contexts are Indian (canal boat and plough, Patiala weightlifting camp, village-fair stone, carrom, goods wagons in a yard, auto-rickshaw, electric bus on a hill road, pinball and dart-gun springs, farm pump, electricity bill in rupees). The CBSE 9 and 10 Science chapters and ICSE 10 Physics "Work, Energy and Power" were read for set-ups: the porter, sledge, lawn-mower, stair-climbing, crane, coconut, flower-pot and pendulum items there are not reused.
