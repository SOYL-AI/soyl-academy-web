## Sources (all SOYL-original)
A caravan-town merchant's account book (around 800 CE), a schoolmistress's diary in the west of Ireland (1847), a Spanish friar's letter from New Spain (1521), a soldier's notebook on an El Dorado expedition (around 1540), a Calicut spice broker's letter to Aden (1525), and a canteen menu table of crop origins. Each stem says it is illustrative and not a real document; the details are period-true (silk and pottery west, silver east, a Buddhist monk on the road; blight and emigration; a smallpox epidemic months before the siege of Tenochtitlan in 1521; Portuguese passes demanded of Indian ships; the gold-dust king of the El Dorado legend).
